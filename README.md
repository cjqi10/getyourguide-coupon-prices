- Language
- Pickup and drop-off service
- Entry ticket inclusion
- Skip-the-line access

## The best way to compare a coupon with an existing sale

A GetYourGuide coupon may not always produce the lowest final price. Some activities already have a reduced price, and some codes cannot be combined with an activity-level promotion.

Use this comparison method:

### 1. Record the normal total

Choose the exact date, number of travelers, and option you need. Note the full checkout total before entering a coupon.

### 2. Check whether the listing is already discounted

Look for a crossed-out price, a percentage reduction, or a special-offer label. Make sure you are comparing the same activity option rather than a cheaper basic version.

### 3. Apply the coupon

Enter the code at checkout and check the final amount. A discount that applies only to the base ticket may not reduce optional extras.

### 4. Check cancellation terms

A slightly more expensive ticket with free cancellation may be more practical than a cheaper non-refundable option, especially for flights and multi-city trips.

### 5. Compare the per-person cost

For group bookings, calculate the final price per traveler. Some options are priced per person, while private tours may be priced per group.

The most useful number is the amount you will actually pay for the exact booking. A large percentage printed beside a coupon is less important if it applies to a different activity or cannot be used with your account.

## Can you cancel a GetYourGuide booking?

Cancellation depends on the individual activity and supplier terms. GetYourGuide’s general terms state that, unless different conditions are shown in the activity description, supplier terms, or voucher, cancellations made more than 24 hours before the activity may qualify for a full refund. Cancellations within 24 hours and no-shows generally do not qualify under that default rule.

Do not treat the 24-hour rule as universal. Some activities use stricter conditions, while others allow more flexible cancellation. The exact policy should be visible before payment and on the booking confirmation.

Check these details before using a coupon:

- Whether the activity is refundable
- The cancellation deadline and time zone
- Whether the deadline is based on the activity start time
- Whether changing the date counts as a cancellation
- Whether a refund returns to the original payment method
- Whether the refund is issued as a GetYourGuide gift card
- Whether a non-refundable option is cheaper

If a booking is canceled, GetYourGuide’s terms say refunds are generally returned to the original payment method, although refund gift cards may be available in some circumstances.

A coupon normally reduces the booking price; it does not remove the need to follow the activity’s cancellation rules.
